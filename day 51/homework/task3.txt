transition-property

Specifies which CSS property should be animated.

transition-duration

Sets how long the transition should take.

Accepts seconds (s) or milliseconds (ms).

transition-timing-function

Defines the speed curve of the transition (how it accelerates or decelerates).

Common values: linear, ease, ease-in, ease-out, ease-in-out.

transition-delay

Sets a delay before the transition starts.

Accepts seconds (s) or milliseconds (ms).
